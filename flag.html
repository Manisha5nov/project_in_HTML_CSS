About Independence Day

Celebrating the spirit of a free India

India's Independence Day is celebrated every year on 15 August. On this historic day in 1947, India became independent from British rule.


Independence Day reminds us of the sacrifices made by our freedom fighters and inspires every citizen to contribute towards the development and unity of our nation.
